# MBB Slide Creation

Build consulting-grade decks using McKinsey storylining, lede structure, content delivery, and layout geometry. See [references/mckinsey-design-spec.md](references/mckinsey-design-spec.md) for exact coordinates, hex codes, and content templates.

## 1. Storylining & Deck Flow

- **Follow the 4-stage deck arc:** Cover → **Executive Synthesis** (`3–4` theme cards on Slide 2) → **Agenda / Section Divider** (`Top Left`, active section highlighted) → **Numbered Evidence Slides** (`1.`, `2A.`, `(1/3)`).
- **Use a 3-slide mini-arc per topic:** open with a *Framing / Scope* slide (the `N` drivers or indicators), prove each driver on *Quantified Evidence* slides (`1` insight per slide), and close on *Implications / Actions*.
- **Draft the action-title storyboard first:** write Slide 1 to Slide N titles as complete sentences; verify the titles alone read as a coherent executive summary.
- **Keep readers oriented with trackers:** prefix slide titles with theme numbers (`1.`, `2A.`), append `(1/3)` on multi-slide series, and place a short status/topic kicker at top (`y < 0.25"`).

## 2. Ledes, Subtitles & Content Delivery

- **Make the action title the lede:** state `Subject + Active Verb + Quantified So-What` (`"TRACTS could enable manufacturers to achieve over 8% cost savings"`), never a topic label.
- **Use the subtitle (`y = 0.99"`) to scope the slide:** state the exact lens, unit, or context (`"Explaining how the model made a single prediction"`), and label every chart with `Metric, Unit` (`"Share of production exported, Percent"`).
- **Deliver prose as `[Bold Lead-in]: [1–2 line insight]`:** structure text blocks as scannable cards (`Context & Challenges` → `Solution` → `Impact`, or `Theme:` + headline + proof), never unstyled bullet walls.
- **Anchor numbers with relatable scale:** pair key metrics with a callout translating abstract numbers into concrete terms (`"~72M acres — the size of Iowa & Illinois combined"`).
- **Cite every data slide:** place `Source: <Primary Data>, McKinsey analysis` at bottom-left in `7–8 pt` Arial.

## 3. Canvas, Grid & Typography

- **Use 16:9 widescreen (`13.33" × 7.50"`):** apply `0.61"` left/right margins for a `12.12"` content width.
- **Pair Georgia + Arial:** use **Georgia** for cover titles (`24–28 pt`), section dividers, and kickers (`10–16 pt`); use **Arial** for action titles (`14–16 pt` bold), body/cards (`10–14 pt`), exhibit labels (`8–9 pt`), and footnotes (`7–8 pt`).
- **Anchor on the McKinsey palette:** Deep Navy (`#051C2C`) for contrast slides/headers, Electric Blue (`#2251FF`) for active highlights, Cyan (`#00A9F4`) for secondary accents, and `#E6E6E6` / `#EDF6F2` for borderless card fills with thin accent bars.

## 4. Layout Archetypes

| Layout | Split / Geometry | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Contrast Title** | `#051C2C` fill, Georgia title at `x=0.60", y=2.24"` | Cover slide with title, subtitle, and date |
| **Top Left / Agenda** | Left rail (`3.79"–4.17"`) + right `2/3` numbered list | Table of contents and section dividers (highlight active section in `#2251FF`) |
| **Default** | Full `12.12"` width exhibit | Executive theme cards, wide chart, or `3–4` equal comparison columns |
| **1/2 Split** | Two `5.85"` columns (`0.42"` gutter) | Before vs. After, Pain Points vs. Solutions, or paired charts |
| **1/3 + 2/3** | Left `3.79"` thesis column + right `7.90"` exhibit | Narrative framing on left paired with detailed breakdown on right |
| **3/4 + 1/4** | Left `8.80"` chart/table + right `2.90"` shaded panel | Data exhibit paired with a `"Key takeaways"` sidebar |
| **Contrast Default** | `#051C2C` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `#00A9F4` metrics | Executive summary, impact headline, or closing next steps |
