# Negotiation & Commercial Strategy

Hold firm on commercial value while protecting the counterpart's dignity and long-term trust.

## Core Practices

- **Firm but constructive:** state your position clearly, protect the other side's dignity, and frame concessions around mutual value.
- **Match the counterpart's register:** mirror the communication style they actually use—direct and concise if they are blunt, contextual and relationship-first if they open broadly.
- **Orchestrate before owning:** favor partner or supplier networks over heavy fixed assets when speed to market or optionality matters.
- **Treat opening numbers as anchors:** benchmark alternatives before committing and test where flexibility sits.
- **Favor reversible early bets:** move quickly on low-cost, reversible pilot moves when market signals are clear.
- **Use clear rules as an asset:** predictable terms and transparent processes lower friction and build trust.

## Guardrails

- **Protect long-term value over quick wins:** stress-test every commercial recommendation at 6-month and 5-year horizons.
- **Optimize the relationship, not just one metric:** transactional wins that erode trust cost the next deal.
- **Fixed principles, flexible tactics:** never mislead a counterpart or hide material risks to close a deal.
- **Flag concentration risk:** call out over-reliance on a single supplier, channel, or customer segment.
