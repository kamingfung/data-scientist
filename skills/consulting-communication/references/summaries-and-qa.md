# Slide Patterns & Q&A Playbook

## 1. Transforming Raw Data into Executive Slides

| Raw input | Upgrade to | Why it works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Multi-column raw dollar table | `% YoY growth` and `% share` bar/dot chart | Exposes relative winners and structural shifts immediately |
| Long bulleted trade-off list | `2x2` matrix (e.g., Impact vs. Effort, Value vs. Risk) | Forces prioritization and reveals the winning quadrant |
| Sequential process prose | Upfront scorecard matrix (Options × Criteria) | Lets executives verify the recommendation against weights on Slide 1 |
| Multi-metric wall of numbers | One chart per slide + 1-line "So What?" title | Prevents cognitive overload and keeps attention on the decision |

## 2. Slide Title Rewrite Check

- **Weak (label):** `Q3 Customer Churn Overview`
- **Strong (conclusion):** `Enterprise churn doubled to 8.4% in Q3 due to onboarding delays`
- **Rule:** If you read only the slide titles from Slide 1 to Slide 12, they must form a complete, coherent executive summary.

## 3. Punchy Q&A Formula (4 Steps)

1. **Answer first (Yes / No / Direct metric):** give the headline answer in the first sentence without restating the question.
2. **Bridge to your core point:** connect the answer to one of your three supporting pillars (`"That comes down to two drivers…"`).
3. **Give one concrete proof point:** back the pillar with one quantified fact or short analogy (Scorsese Principle).
4. **Stop talking (No-Tailgating):** pause cleanly instead of trailing off with caveats or over-explaining.

## 4. Handling Difficult Questions

- **Never repeat a negative premise:** reframe onto the positive goal or factual baseline before answering.
- **Separate unknown facts from known boundaries:** if data is missing, state what is known, what is unknown, and the exact next check to resolve it.

## 5. Executive Status Updates & Stakeholder Alignment

- **Status Update Format:**
  - **Status / Decision:** 1 sentence (`On track` / `At risk` / `Needs decision: X`).
  - **What Changed:** 2–3 quantified bullets since the last update.
  - **Blockers & Next Actions:** `[Owner] — [Action] by [YYYY-MM-DD]`.
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** anchor on shared business objectives, separate people from options, and quantify trade-offs (cost, timeline, risk) before recommending a path.
